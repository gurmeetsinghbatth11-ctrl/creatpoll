About PollSpace

PollSpace is a simple online polling platform that lets anyone create a question, add answer options and share the poll with other people.

People can open a shared poll link, vote for an option and view the results. Poll results can update automatically as new votes are submitted.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make online polling simple, fast and easy to share.

How It Works

Create a poll, add your options, publish it and share the generated link. Other people can open the link and vote.

PollSpace is designed to be simple enough for everyday questions, opinions, feedback and informal surveys.
